Neutral ramp (ICC) · how the press actually hits a grey
Each cell starts from a target sRGB grey (e.g. 50% grey), gets reverse-routed to CMYK through the active profile (the press's GCR / UCR rules pick the CMY + K mix), then forward-routed back to screen sRGB. This is the grey the RIP gives you when you ask the press for a neutral.
Pure RGB reference · rgb(255-2.55*K, K = 0→100)
Each cell is plain sRGB grey: rgb(v, v, v) where v = 255 - 2.55*K. No profile routing - K=100 is exact #000, K=0 is exact #FFF. This is the design-intent baseline (what your editor shows when you type "50% grey"). Compare against the ICC-neutral row above to see how far the press drifts from ideal black.
